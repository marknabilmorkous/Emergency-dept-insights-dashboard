# Emergency Department Performance Dashboard

An interactive healthcare analytics dashboard designed to monitor, visualize, and optimize Emergency Department (ED) operations, patient flow, and financial performance.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Overview
<img width="1342" height="817" alt="Dashboard  " src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/26ee23e7-f263-425e-803d-27430c12f12d" />


The **Emergency Department Performance Dashboard** provides high-level key performance indicators (KPIs) alongside detailed categorical breakdowns to help hospital administrators identify operational bottlenecks, patient demographic trends, and revenue drivers.

### Key Metrics Monitored
* **Total Patients:** 1.200K total patient admissions recorded.
* **Average Wait Time:** 47 minutes average wait time across all triage levels.
* **Readmission Rate:** Overall 14.7% 30-day readmission rate.
* **Total Revenue:** $1.81M generated across treated ED emergency cases.

### Core Visualizations & Breakdown
1. **Wait Time by Triage Level:** Analyzes average wait times across triage severity levels:
   * **Level 3 (Urgent):** 128 mins (Primary bottleneck)
   * **Level 4 (Less Urgent):** 126 mins
   * **Level 5 (Non-Urgent):** 98 mins
   * **Level 2 (Emergent):** 44 mins
   * **Level 1 (Resuscitation):** 9 mins
2. **Patient by Insurance Type:** Categorizes patient volume by coverage type (Private Insurance: 51%, Government/Medicaid: 35%, Self-Pay/Uninsured: 14%).
3. **Readmission Rate by Age Group:** Highlights readmission trends peaking at 24.6% for patients aged 80+.
4. **Patient Admission Matrix:** Tracks patient ID, age, chief complaints (e.g., Fever/Infection, Chest Pain, Abdominal Pain), and wait time.

---

## 💡 Insights & Business Recommendations

* **Waiting Bottlenecks:** Level 3 (Urgent) candidates face an average wait time of 128 minutes, indicating a need for additional staff scheduling during peak surge hours.
* **Readmission Prevention:** High readmission rates among elderly patients (>65 years old) with chronic conditions (diabetes and heart disease) suggest implementing a dedicated post-discharge home follow-up program.
* **Financial Return:** Critical cases represent 25% of overall patient volume but generate **60% of total emergency revenue**.
